# Autodrive Live — original assets

The Atlas S4, Atlas Touring and Metro C2 are original unbranded vehicle models generated by `js/vehicles.js`: smooth lofted bodywork, window surrounds, pillars, doors, mirrors, grilles, squared lamps, seven-spoke wheels and original plates. Paint, glass, wheels and lights use matte materials. No imported vehicle models are loaded in this version.

The city, district layouts, buildings, facades, asphalt, paving, trees, bridges, canal, furniture, road markings and navigation graphics are generated locally by this project. The districts are fictional.

Three.js and its utilities are MIT licensed: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

Interface fonts: Barlow Condensed and DM Sans, distributed by Google Fonts under their respective Open Font Licenses. System fonts are used as fallbacks.

The previous prototype vehicle and its attribution have been moved to `artifacts/` as a local archive. They are not served or used by the game. Autodrive Live is independent of TikTok and any car manufacturer.
