# Last City asset generation

Created with the built-in **imagegen** tool on 2026-11-25. The final project asset is `public/assets/kingdom-atlas.png` (1254 × 1254 RGBA). The generator chose the output dimensions; runtime cell boundaries use rounded image quarters rather than assuming 512-pixel cells. The source concept was the user's blue-and-gold castle / zombie-defense reference. No third-party sprite sheet was downloaded.

The first output had a white background, so a second imagegen pass extracted the background. The final output was inspected in the game and in `/assets.html`. The loader slices all sixteen cells, scans alpha once to trim padding, and caches each crop. There is no image generation at runtime.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite atlas for a medieval castle zombie defense browser game named The Last City. Create ONE 2048 by 2048 transparent PNG sprite sheet with an EXACT 4 column by 4 row grid, 16 equally sized 512x512 cells. Each object completely isolated in its own cell with generous transparent padding, never crossing cell borders. No text, labels, grid lines or terrain platforms. Consistent beautiful high quality pre-rendered 3D isometric strategy game look, detailed chunky beveled stone and wood, royal blue roofs, gold trims, green foliage. Camera orthographic isometric view with visible front and right side, soft lighting from upper left. All objects align bottom center inside cells. Row 1: cell 1 a grand complete blue-roof gold-trim medieval castle keep with several turrets and a gate (fully inside its cell); cell 2 cozy blue roof timber cottage; cell 3 medieval windmill blue roof wood sails; cell 4 blue-roof stone barracks with swords sign. Row 2: cell 1 a cluster of tall dark green fir trees; cell 2 a round leafy green oak tree; cell 3 a stone cylindrical crenellated defense tower without roof; cell 4 a gold and steel cannon on wood wheels aimed toward upper left. Row 3 characters must be LARGE in their own cells, full body with no platform: cell 1 blue and gold archer facing away three quarter toward upper right with drawn bow; cell 2 blue and gold armored captain knight with red plume facing away three quarter upper left; cell 3 medieval worker brown clothes carrying wood walking facing lower right; cell 4 blue-robed mage with glowing ice staff facing upper left. Row 4 full body enemies facing viewer three quarter lower left: cell 1 green zombie ragged clothes arms forward; cell 2 thin green runner goblin leaning forward; cell 3 bulky green orc with steel shoulder armor; cell 4 enormous horned green undead boss with spiked club. These are individual game assets with true transparent empty background, not a scene or illustration. Every cell has one clearly separated asset with same consistent visual style. Sharp clean readable silhouettes, detailed but no outlines, no ground tiles, no logos.

## Background extraction prompt

Background extraction ONLY. Remove the white background from this entire 4 by 4 sprite sheet. The current input white background is opaque: it must become genuinely fully transparent alpha=0 in all white empty areas around all sixteen sprites. Preserve exactly all sixteen assets, their positions, dimensions, colors and details. Do NOT redraw, reposition, crop, add a checkerboard, or add a background. Keep the 4 by 4 grid arrangement exactly identical. Output true transparent RGBA PNG with every sprite a clean isolated cutout.

## Animation scope

The atlas has single poses. Runtime transforms provide bobbing, recoil, facing and falling corpses. It does not provide separate limb layers, a Spine skeleton or full directional walk/attack frame sequences. The oak, knight and mage are inspected in the gallery and reserved for future gameplay; thirteen cells currently appear in the game.
